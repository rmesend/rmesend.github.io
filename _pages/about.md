                                    I am **Roger Mesén-Delgado**, a Costa Rican data scientist and geospatial analyst with over 5 years of experience at the intersection of technology, agriculture, and spatial analysis. I currently serve as **Smart Farming Manager at [Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc.](https://www.freshdelmonte.com/)**, integrating earth observations, satellite imagery, field data, and real-time streams to optimize agricultural decisions and enhance operational sustainability.

                                    My work blends data analysis with ESRI GIS tools (ArcGIS Pro, Survey123), Oracle databases, and Python libraries including **Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-Learn** for predictive modeling, dashboard development, and exploratory data analysis applied to precision agriculture.

                                    Prior to my current role, I worked as a **GIS Specialist at Geotecnologías S.A. (ESRI Costa Rica)**, designing and maintaining geospatial databases, producing cartography, and supporting technical teams in GIS application deployment.

                                    I hold a **Bachelor's in Anthropology** from the University of Costa Rica — a background that provides a unique lens for analyzing territorial and social systems. I complement this with an ongoing **Master's in Geography** (UCR) and a **Bachelor's in Software Engineering** (UNED), alongside a **Machine Learning Certification** (PROMIDAT), a **Data Science Specialization** (Coding Dojo), an **ArcGIS Desktop Entry** certification from ESRI, and Google's **Foundations of Project Management**.

                                    I am currently enrolled in the **Project Management Techniques and Tools (GP-0204)** course at the **Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica**, deepening my expertise in **PMBoK®** methodology, MS Project®, and network analysis for professional project management.

                                    I have contributed to archaeological research in Costa Rica, exploring pre-Hispanic and colonial routes in the Caribbean region, published in the journal *Vínculos* (2016). I share knowledge through articles and collaborate on open-source data science and GIS projects on GitHub.
