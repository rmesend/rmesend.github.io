I am Roger Mesén-Delgado, a Costa Rican data scientist and geospatial analyst with over 5 years of professional experience at the intersection of technology, agriculture, and spatial analysis.

**Professional Experience**

Since October 2019, I have been working at Fresh Del Monte Produce Inc. as Smart Farming Manager (Superintendent level), where I lead the implementation of precision agriculture technologies across large-scale tropical fruit production operations. My work involves integrating GIS tools such as ArcGIS Pro and Survey123 with data-driven workflows to optimize field operations, monitor crop health, and support agronomic decision-making. Prior to this role, I worked as a GIS Specialist at Geotecnologías S.A. (ESRI Costa Rica) from October 2017 to October 2019, providing geospatial consulting and technical support for a range of clients across Central America.

**Skills & Technologies**

My technical toolkit spans several domains. On the geospatial side, I work extensively with ArcGIS Pro, Survey123, and related ESRI products. For data science and analysis, I use Python with libraries including Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Scikit-Learn. I also have experience with Oracle Database for enterprise data management, and I am comfortable working across the full data pipeline from collection to visualization and modeling.

**Education**

My academic background is deliberately interdisciplinary. I hold a Bachelor's degree in Anthropology from the Universidad de Costa Rica (UCR), which gave me a strong foundation in research methods and critical thinking. I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Geography at UCR and a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at the Universidad Estatal a Distancia (UNED), both in progress. I have also completed a Machine Learning certification at PROMIDAT, a Data Science specialization at Coding Dojo, the ArcGIS Desktop Entry certification from ESRI, and the Foundations of Project Management course from Google.

Most recently, I began the course *Técnicas y Herramientas para la Gerencia de Proyectos (GP-0204)* at the Instituto Tecnológico de Costa Rica (TEC), currently in Week 2. The course covers project management methodologies including PMBoK®, scheduling tools such as MS Project® and Excel, and techniques for process planning, monitoring, and network analysis.

**Research & Publications**

In 2016, I co-authored a research article published in the *Vínculos* journal exploring pre-Hispanic and colonial routes in the Caribbean region of Costa Rica. This work combined archaeological fieldwork with spatial analysis to reconstruct historical mobility patterns, reflecting my long-standing interest in applying geospatial methods to humanistic research questions.

**What I Do Outside of Work**

I regularly share technical notes and reflections on Medium, covering topics such as spatial data analysis, Python for GIS, and smart farming. I also maintain open-source projects on GitHub centered on data science and geospatial workflows. I believe in learning in public and documenting the practical side of working with data in agricultural and environmental contexts.
